v3.1: Goalie/striker coordination in our penalty area (striker no longer charges the ball together with the goalie; it covers the far post while the goalie plays it) and ryc's near-post goalie depth (for shots heading near a post the goalie drops level with the posts so deflections go out, not in). Both switchable in config.yaml (strategy.goalie_priority / strategy.goalie_post_level). Developed and checked in the Booster Studio 3v3 simulator; NOT yet tested on a real robot.
v3.0: Rebased on Booster K1_5v5_demo_1.7 (firmware 1.7+). Striker fixes for "spins/orbits before kicking" and "approaches the ball too fast": near-ball speed limit on (0.5 m/s within 2 m), brain ball confidence 60->45, tighter vertical head tracking, back up instead of spinning when the ball is lost at the feet, kick-decision hysteresis, kick abort ball-lost timeout 1000->2000 ms (configurable). v2.x patches (built on demo 1.6) are NOT carried over yet.
